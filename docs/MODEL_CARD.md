# PhishGuard Evidence model card

## Intended use

This is a research and teaching baseline for **human-assisted review** of
phishing-like email text. It must not automatically block messages, delete
email, label people, or make consequential security decisions.

## Model and data

- Architecture: TF-IDF unigrams/bigrams (up to 60,000 features) followed by
  class-weighted logistic regression.
- Source: [`zefang-liu/phishing-email-dataset`](https://huggingface.co/datasets/zefang-liu/phishing-email-dataset)
  at revision `34085a032c123ca237f314a01a67909cdea35e34`.
- Positive class: records whose dataset `Email Type` contains “phishing”. This
  may include spam-like messages and is not a perfect label for targeted attacks.
- Preprocessing: remove empty/short/overlong records and exact duplicates after
  whitespace normalization and case folding.
- Cleaned dataset fingerprint: `176d653fcc961415d0b93e9460b5c2f4bee181c4c79ab80cb805ce0540765506`.
- Split: seeded (`42`) stratified random 80/20; 13,980 train and 3,495 test.

## Failure modes and safeguards

- Near-duplicate campaigns can cross a random split and inflate apparent
  generalization. Evaluate on new campaigns and time periods before use.
- Text-only analysis misses headers, authentication signals, redirect chains,
  attachments, and mailbox context.
- The model's `predict_proba` output is not separately calibrated. The
  demonstration bands below 0.35 and above 0.65 are not operational thresholds.
- Term weights show statistical associations. They can be counterintuitive and
  are not causal explanations or evidence of malicious intent.
- Email text may contain personal or confidential information. The local CLI
  prints the analyzed evidence packet; do not expose it in public logs or UIs.
- The forensic scanner only reads URL strings. It never opens, resolves, or
  follows them.

## Required next evaluation

Use campaign-, sender-, and time-separated test sets; report calibration,
false-positive cost, multilingual and obfuscated-message performance, and
reviewer agreement. Integrate with a documented incident response process
and require human approval before any action.
